# ACoolAGENT User Onboarding Framework

This document outlines the complete user onboarding process for the ACoolAGENT platform, designed to provide a seamless introduction to Los Angeles business registration assistance.

## Onboarding Goals

1. **Collect essential user information** to personalize the business registration experience
2. **Assess current business stage** to provide relevant guidance and resources
3. **Set clear expectations** about platform capabilities and support
4. **Establish communication preferences** across WhatsApp, email, and web portal
5. **Create initial business registration timeline** tailored to user's specific needs

## Onboarding Channels

The onboarding process is available through three primary channels:

### 1. WhatsApp Business

- **Initial contact point** for most users
- **Conversational assessment** through guided questions
- **Document submission** via mobile camera
- **Real-time assistance** for immediate questions

### 2. Web Portal

- **Comprehensive onboarding form** with all assessment questions
- **Secure document upload** functionality
- **Dashboard setup** with progress tracking
- **Calendar integration** for agency appointments

### 3. In-Person Assistance

- Available at **BusinessSource Centers**
- **Guided digital onboarding** with staff assistance
- **Document scanning and verification**
- **Immediate consultation** on next steps

## Onboarding Flow

### Step 1: Initial Contact & Welcome

**Trigger:** User initiates contact via WhatsApp or web portal

**Content:**
- Welcome message explaining ACoolAGENT's purpose
- Overview of onboarding process (5-10 minutes)
- Privacy policy and terms of service acceptance
- Option to schedule in-person assistance

**User Action Required:** Confirm interest in proceeding

### Step 2: Business Profile Creation

**Content:**
- Basic personal information collection:
  - Name
  - Contact information
  - Preferred communication channel
- Business concept information:
  - Proposed business name
  - Business type/industry
  - Location considerations

**User Action Required:** Complete profile information

### Step 3: Business Stage Assessment

**Content:**
- Current stage in business development:
  - Concept/planning
  - Ready for registration
  - Partially registered
  - Seeking permits
  - Funding stage
  - Launch preparation
- Current document status:
  - What documents are already secured
  - What registrations are complete

**User Action Required:** Complete assessment questionnaire

### Step 4: Document Collection & Verification

**Content:**
- Required document checklist based on assessment
- Instructions for secure document upload
- Verification process explanation
- Missing document guidance

**User Action Required:** Upload available documents

### Step 5: Personalized Roadmap Creation

**Content:**
- Generated business registration timeline
- Agency touchpoint schedule
- Cost estimation for registration process
- Funding opportunity identification

**User Action Required:** Review and confirm roadmap

### Step 6: Communication Setup

**Content:**
- Notification preferences configuration
- Calendar integration for important dates
- Agency appointment scheduling preferences
- Progress tracking method selection

**User Action Required:** Configure communication preferences

### Step 7: Next Steps & Activation

**Content:**
- Immediate next steps based on assessment
- First agency interaction preparation
- Dashboard access instructions
- Support contact information

**User Action Required:** Confirm understanding and schedule first action

## Content Requirements

### Welcome Message

```
Welcome to ACoolAGENT! 👋

I'm your Los Angeles business navigation assistant. I'll help you:

✅ Register your business correctly
✅ Save time with government agencies
✅ Meet all legal requirements
✅ Access funding opportunities

Let's start with a quick 5-minute assessment to personalize your business journey.

Ready to begin?
```

### Business Assessment Questions

The assessment collects critical information through a conversational interface:

1. **Business Concept**
   - "Tell me about the business you want to start."
   - "What industry are you in?"
   - "Where in Los Angeles are you planning to operate?"

2. **Current Progress**
   - "Have you started any registration process yet?"
   - "Do you have an EIN (Employer Identification Number)?"
   - "Have you registered your business name?"

3. **Timeline & Budget**
   - "When do you hope to officially launch your business?"
   - "What budget have you allocated for registration and permits?"
   - "Are you seeking funding assistance?"

4. **Support Needs**
   - "What aspects of business registration seem most challenging to you?"
   - "Do you need language assistance for any agency interactions?"
   - "Would you prefer in-person support at certain stages?"

### Document Request Messages

```
Based on your business type and stage, you'll need these documents:

📄 Government-issued ID
📄 Proof of address
📄 Business concept description
📄 [Additional documents based on assessment]

You can securely upload these through this chat or our web portal. Need help gathering any of these?
```

### Roadmap Presentation

```
Here's your personalized Los Angeles business registration roadmap:

Week 1: BusinessSource Center visit & EIN application
Week 2-3: Business name registration with County
Week 3-4: City tax registration & permit assessment
Week 5-6: [Specific permits based on business type]
Week 7-8: Funding application & bank account setup

We'll guide you through each step with reminders and specific instructions. Does this timeline work for you?
```

## Technical Requirements

### Integration Points

- **WhatsApp Business API** for message handling
- **Document Processing System** for verification
- **Agency Database** for requirements and contacts
- **Calendar Systems** for appointment scheduling
- **Email Service** for notifications and updates

### Security Considerations

- **End-to-End Encryption** for all communications
- **Secure Document Storage** with access controls
- **Authentication Systems** for web portal access
- **Data Retention Policies** compliant with regulations
- **Audit Logging** for all system interactions

## Onboarding Performance Metrics

The following metrics will be tracked to assess and improve the onboarding process:

1. **Completion Rate:** Percentage of users who complete all onboarding steps
2. **Time to Completion:** Average time taken to complete onboarding
3. **Abandonment Points:** Steps where users most frequently drop off
4. **Document Submission Rate:** Percentage of requested documents uploaded
5. **Support Requests:** Number and types of assistance needed during onboarding
6. **Roadmap Acceptance:** Percentage of users who confirm proposed timeline
7. **Channel Preference:** Distribution of users across onboarding channels
