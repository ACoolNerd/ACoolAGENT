# ACoolAGENT Seed Funding Strategy

## Executive Summary

This document outlines the comprehensive strategy for securing $750,000 in seed funding to develop and launch the ACoolAGENT Minimum Viable Product (MVP). The funding will support a cross-functional team over 9 months to build, test, and deploy the initial platform connecting entrepreneurs to Los Angeles business registration resources through WhatsApp, web interfaces, and in-person support.

## Risk Mitigation Strategy

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|------------|--------|------------|
| Delayed grant decisions | Medium | High | Stagger applications across different timelines |
| Lower than expected award amounts | Medium | Medium | Modular development approach with prioritized features |
| Restrictive funding terms | Low | High | Thorough review of all terms before acceptance |
| Founder dilution concerns | Medium | Medium | Cap table modeling with various scenarios |
| Extended runway needs | High | High | Revenue model activation within 6 months of launch |
